## Gabriel de Almeida Santos Silva

Founder of **PoWV Protocol**. I research how a physical event (a weight, a measurement, a custody handover) can become attributable, verifiable digital evidence, and how that evidence can safely inform operational and financial decisions.

> A signature can show that a record is intact. It cannot show that the physical event happened as recorded. PoWV keeps those two claims separate.

## What exists today

| Date | Result | Record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sep 2026 | Selected software validation: signing, verification, replay control, local audit | [Validation evidence](https://powv-protocol.gitbook.io/powv-protocol-docs/project-status/validation-evidence) |
| Sep 2026 | Bench test: a real scale reading captured, identified and delivered to an ESP32 receiver | [Experimental record](https://github.com/powvprotocol-org/powv-scale-edge-integration/blob/main/docs/integration-module/experimental-record.md) |
| Nov 2026 | Software hardening, cross-language conformance, audit integrity; 343 automated tests passing | [Lab status](https://github.com/powvprotocol-org/powv-lab-status) |

**Stage:** research and controlled laboratory validation. No product is commercially available, and no hardware-backed identity or field pilot has been demonstrated yet.

**Next:** a physical bench test of the signed capture path, then a hardware secure element, then a controlled industrial pilot.
